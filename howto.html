How to Use Hunt Link

The basics in five minutes. Questions? See Help or email huntlinkinfo@gmail.com.

Before you leave home: join your group, then save the map for offline. Zoom the map to your hunt area and tap ⬇ on the right. Wait for "Offline map saved." Do it on every phone in your party.

1. Start or join a group

  • Start one: type your name and a group name, then tap Create group. Hunt Link makes a code like HL-7K3P-Q9XD.
  • Send the code: on the GROUP tab, tap Send code only (just the code, easy to copy) or Send invite (a short message with the code). You can also press and hold the code to copy it.
  • Join one: type your name, paste the code (or the whole invite message) under Join a group, and tap Join with code.
  • No group? Tap Use on my own. Everything stays on your phone.

2. The map buttons

  • Maps: pick Trail, Topo or Satellite, and turn on 3D terrain, forest roads or your breadcrumb trail.
  • Rec: records your track, even with the phone locked in your pocket. Tap Stop when done. Tracks save under WPTS.
  • Draw: drag a finger to circle or mark something. Pick a color, then tap Done.
  • Dist: tap anywhere on the map to see how far it is from you and which way.
  • GPS: type or paste coordinates to find a spot, or share your own.
  • ⬇: saves the map on your screen for use with no signal.
  • ◎: puts the map back on you.
  • Compass dial (top left): tap it for the big compass. Tap again to close it.

3. Waypoints and getting there

  • Drop a waypoint: press and hold on the map. Name it, pick an icon, and choose Group sees it or Only my phone.
  • Walk to it: on the WPTS tab, tap Nav. A line runs from you to the waypoint, and the big compass shows which way to turn and when you're on line.
  • Drive to it: after you tap Nav, tap Drive to get directions in Google Maps or Apple Maps. For a spot from coordinates, use GPS, then Drive there. Roads may be closed, private or rough.
  • Share it: tap Share, then send a Hunt Link link, Google Maps text, or a GPX file for onX, Gaia or Google Earth.

4. The tabs at the bottom

  • HOME: the map.
  • GROUP: your group code, who's in it, how far away they are, and Leave or Delete my data.
  • WPTS: your waypoints, tracks and drawings.
  • CHAT: group messages. Press and hold a message to report it or block the sender.
  • WX: weather, wind and which direction to approach from.

5. No cell service?

GPS, Rec, the compass, saved waypoints and downloaded maps all work with no signal. Group positions and chat need a signal and catch up when you get one. Don't count on Hunt Link in an emergency: call 911.
