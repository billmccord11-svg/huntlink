Hunt Link Help

Maps, hunt units, group location and chat for your hunting party. New to Hunt Link? Start with How to use Hunt Link.

Need help, or want to report someone? Email huntlinkinfo@gmail.com. A screenshot helps a lot. We answer reports within 24 hours.

How do I start a group?

On the first screen, type your name and a group name (like "Elk Camp 2026"), then tap Create group. Hunt Link makes a secure code like HL-7K3P-Q9XD. Tap Share code to text it to your party. You can share it again any time from the GROUP tab.

How do I join my buddy's group?

Type your name, paste or type the code under Join a group, and tap Join with code. Joining the first time needs a signal. After that, tap Continue to your group on the first screen. That works even with no service.

My old group code stopped working

Hunt Link switched to secure codes, which can't be guessed, and the old codes were retired. One person creates a new group and shares the new code.

How do I report or block someone in chat?

Press and hold their message. Choose Report message to send it to us, or Block to hide their messages and map spot on your phone. You can unblock them in the GROUP tab. The person who created a group can also delete any message in it.

How do I leave a group or delete my data?

Go to the GROUP tab. Leave takes you off that group's map and chat. Delete my data erases your positions, messages and waypoints from our servers and everything Hunt Link saved on your phone. That can't be undone.

The compass seems off

Magnet phone mounts and cases, metal and vehicles throw off any phone's compass. When you're moving faster than a walk, Hunt Link points by your GPS direction of travel instead, and the corner dial shows "GPS." Standing still, take the phone off the mount and wave it in a figure 8.

My track has straight lines in it

Tap Rec before you start walking. Rec keeps recording with the phone locked in your pocket. On Android, you'll see a "Hunt Link is recording your track" notification while it runs. Tap Stop when you're done, and the track saves under WPTS, then Tracks.

Does it work without cell service?

GPS, Rec, the compass, saved waypoints and downloaded maps work with no signal and in airplane mode. To use maps offline, tap ⬇ while you have service to save the area on screen. Group positions and chat need a signal and catch up when you get one.
