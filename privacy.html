Hunt Link Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version: Hunt Link shares your location, chat and waypoints only with the people in your hunt group. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we never sell or share your information. You can erase your data at any time with Delete my data.

Who we are

Hunt Link is made by PinkSisters LLC, an Arizona company ("we" or "us"). Using Hunt Link also means agreeing to our terms of use.

No accounts

You don't sign up or make a password. When you first open Hunt Link, it gets a random private ID for your phone from Google Firebase (anonymous sign-in). That ID is how the app knows which messages and map spots are yours. It is not linked to your name, phone number, email or Apple or Google account.

What your group sees

A group is private: only people who have its code can join. Codes are random, like HL-7K3P-Q9XD, so they can't be guessed. While Hunt Link is open, the people in your group see:

  • Your name, as you typed it.
  • Your position on the map, updated about every 10 seconds while the app is open. Hunt Link does not share your position when the app is closed.
  • Your chat messages, along with where you were when you sent each one.
  • Waypoints you save to the group. (When you save a waypoint, you can pick Only my phone instead.)

This information is stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so your group can see it, including after a spell without signal. Security rules let only group members read it, and only you can change or delete what's yours. The person who created a group can also delete messages and waypoints in it.

What stays on your phone

Recorded tracks (Rec), drawings, waypoints marked on this phone, the breadcrumb trail, downloaded maps, your blocked list and your settings are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us. They leave your phone only if you tap Share and choose where to send them.

You can also use Hunt Link on your own, without a group. Then nothing about your location, waypoints, tracks or drawings is sent to Hunt Link's servers.

Sharing links

When you share a waypoint, track or drawing as a Hunt Link link, the item itself (its name, color and points) is packed into the link. Nothing is stored on our servers. Whoever has the link can see the item, so send it only to people you want to have it. Long tracks are simplified to fit.

Tapping the link opens a page on our website (hosted by GitHub Pages). The item is in the part of the link after the #, which your phone never sends to the website, so the website can't see what was shared. GitHub receives your phone's internet address when the page loads, like any website. If you tap Open in Hunt Link, the item is saved only on your phone.

Location

Hunt Link uses your phone's GPS to show you on the map, share your spot with your group, guide you to waypoints and work out legal shooting light. If you tap Rec, the app keeps recording your track with the screen locked until you tap Stop; while it records, your phone shows that location is in use. That track stays on your phone. Hunt Link never asks for "always" location access.

Chat alerts

If you allow notifications, your phone gets a push address from Apple or Google. Hunt Link saves it in your group so chat messages can buzz your phone. Alerts are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple or Google.

Services Hunt Link uses

  • Google Firebase: anonymous sign-in and the group data described above.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website, including the page a Hunt Link link opens.
  • Mapbox: map images. Your phone asks Mapbox for the part of the map you're looking at. Hunt Link turns off Mapbox's usage tracking.
  • US Geological Survey, US Forest Service, and the Arizona, Colorado and New Mexico wildlife agencies: topo maps, forest roads and hunt unit lines, loaded from their public map services.
  • Open-Meteo: weather. When you open the WX tab, the app sends your approximate location to Open-Meteo to get the forecast.
  • Expo: chat alerts and app updates.
  • Google Maps or Apple Maps: only when you tap Drive there, which opens directions in that app.

Each service receives what it needs to do its job, along with your phone's internet address, under its own privacy policy. Hunt Link has no advertising and no analytics.

Reports and blocking

Press and hold a chat message to report it or block the person who sent it. A report sends us a copy of that message, the name of the person who sent it, and the group it came from, so we can review it. We review reports within 24 hours and can remove content and people who break the rules. Blocking hides that person's messages and map spot on your phone only.

Deleting your data

  • Leave group (GROUP tab) takes your name and map spot out of that group. Your past messages and waypoints stay in it.
  • Delete my data (GROUP tab, or the start screen) erases your positions, messages and waypoints from our servers in every group you joined on that phone, then erases everything Hunt Link saved on the phone and gives it a new private ID. This can't be undone.

If you created a group and you're the last one in it, the group itself is removed too. If others are still in it, the group's name and code stay so they can keep using it. Reports you sent are kept so we can deal with them, and they're never shown to anyone else.

If you no longer have the phone, email us and we'll help.

Children

Hunt Link is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
