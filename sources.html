AntlerPin Map Sources

Updated November 25, 2026

AntlerPin is not a government app. AntlerPin is made by PinkSisters LLC, a private company. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for any government agency. The map information below comes from public government sources, linked here so you can check it at the source.

Public land (nationwide)

Public land shading and the "who manages this land" lookup come from the U.S. Geological Survey's Protected Areas Database of the United States (PAD-US): https://www.usgs.gov/programs/gap-analysis-project/science/pad-us-data-overview

Topo maps

The topo map style uses USGS topographic maps from The National Map: https://www.usgs.gov/programs/national-geospatial-program/national-map

Forest Service roads

Forest roads and trails come from the USDA Forest Service Motor Vehicle Use Maps: https://www.fs.usda.gov/visit/maps

Weather

Weather and wind forecasts come from Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), which builds its forecasts from national weather services, including NOAA's National Weather Service (weather.gov).

Check the official source

Map lines come from these public sources and can be out of date or wrong. They are not proof of a legal boundary or permission to enter land. Always check your state's current hunting regulations and the managing agency before you hunt. In an emergency, call 911.
