Shooting light, weather and other numbers

Legal shooting light times, sunrise and sunset, weather, distances, compass headings and GPS positions are estimates. Phones, mounts, cases, terrain and weather all affect them. Always follow your state's hunting rules and your own judgment. Never take a shot based only on a time or position shown in the app.

Safety and emergencies

  • Hunt Link is not an emergency service. Group positions and chat need cell service, and alerts can be late or never arrive. In an emergency, call 911.
  • Carry a paper map, a compass or GPS unit, and a way to call for help. Tell someone where you're going.
  • Don't use Hunt Link while driving. Drive there opens Google Maps or Apple Maps. Roads they suggest may be closed, private, rough or impassable.
